ACADEMIC RESOURCE SALES

Creators keep 70%

On a one-time resource purchase, 70% goes to its creator and 30% to Campus360. For Academic Hub subscriptions, 70% of the ₹29 academic portion is shared among creators based on unique paid-resource downloads during that month.

Creator 70%Campus360 30%

Academic subscription creator shares are allocated after the month ends. If no eligible resource is downloaded, no creator pool is generated for that term. Creator earnings unlock after five moderator-approved uploads; payout settlement is not configured yet.
